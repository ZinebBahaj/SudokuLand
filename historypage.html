Complete Sudoku History for Absolute Beginners!

Sudoku ("su" = number, "doku" = single) is a 9x9 grid puzzle divided into nine 3x3 boxes. Fill so every row, column, and box has 1-9 no repeats. Given numbers ("givens") start you off. Goal: logic, not guessing.

Early Roots: Latin Square puzzles (1700s Euler). Modern form by Howard Garns (1979 USA) in "Number Place" magazine. Nikoli Japan (1984) made symmetric version, named "Sudoku", sold millions.

Global Explosion: 2004-2005 UK Times featured it - newspapers worldwide followed. Books, apps, Guinness records (fastest 1:23!). World Sudoku Championship since 2006.
